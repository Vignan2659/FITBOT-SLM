# Yoga photo credits

The yoga photos in FitBot are real photos of people. Each pose has one to three photos, listed in motion order (the
starting position first, the finished pose last), so the app can play them as a short motion when the cursor is on the
picture. They were placed on a common 3:2 canvas and resized for the web app (frontend/public/yoga/photos/); the
originals are on the linked source pages. The app shows the same credit under each photo on the pose pages.

* Studio photos: [Margaret Young](https://unsplash.com/@wildsoulmagsyogi) on Unsplash, under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) (free to use; credit given anyway).
* Gym photos: the public domain [free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db) set, the same photos as the exercise section.
